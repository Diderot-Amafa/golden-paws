Our Story

A family breeding
with passion

Since 2014, our breeding has been exclusively dedicated to the Golden Retriever. Passionate about this breed, we carefully select our breeding dogs to guarantee puppies in perfect health with exemplary temperament.

Our puppies grow up in our home, in daily contact with our children and animals. This early socialization is essential for forming balanced and confident dogs.

  • ✓ Puppies registered with the Livre des Origines Français (LOF)
  • ✓ Complete veterinary health check before departure
  • ✓ Vaccines, deworming and microchip included
  • ✓ Lifetime follow-up and support
  • ✓ 2-year contractual health warranty
